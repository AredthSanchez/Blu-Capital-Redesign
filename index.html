Which financing option should I explore?

Use the business-needs guide above to find a starting point. Then discuss your goal, timing, and financial situation with Blu’s team to understand the available options.

What should I prepare for a conversation?

Be ready to describe your business, the amount and purpose of financing, and your preferred timeline. Ask the team which documents are required for the product you’re considering.

How do I learn about rates and fees?

Request a breakdown of pricing, repayment or collection arrangements, fees, and other conditions for your proposed financing. Review the written terms before making a decision.

Can I speak to someone about a cross-border business?

Yes. Blu serves businesses operating in the U.S., Mexico, or both. Contact the team to discuss your particular operation and financing needs.
